# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineers, AI builders, founders, recruiters, collaborators, and curious GitHub visitors evaluating Divyanshu Goyal's work and point of view. They should understand within a few seconds who he is, what he builds, and what he is building now.

## Product Purpose

Present Divyanshu as an exceptional engineer, AI architect, researcher-builder, and startup founder through a memorable GitHub-native profile. Nnomi is the current founder mission. JARVIS, the DAG ledger, and aviation forecasting provide concise evidence of technical range. Success means the profile feels authored, specific, and worth studying or copying.

## Brand Personality

Intelligent, technical, and mischievous. The voice is confident without corporate polish, witty without becoming a joke wall, and precise without reading like a resume or keyword list.

## Anti-references

Generic cyberpunk, Matrix or gaming imagery, fake terminal theatrics, resume dashboards, badge walls, vanity-stat grids, corporate LinkedIn copy, AI buzzword stacks, and layouts that appear assembled from a template.

## Design Principles

1. Identity lands before metadata.
2. Show engineering evidence instead of listing credentials.
3. Make Nnomi unmistakably the current mission.
4. Reward close reading with restrained, personal details.
5. Edit ruthlessly until every line and visual element earns its place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain distinct dark and light themes, readable contrast, useful alternative text, scalable SVGs, semantic README structure, and legibility around GitHub's typical 850 to 900 px content width. Important meaning must not depend on color, animation, or private telemetry.
